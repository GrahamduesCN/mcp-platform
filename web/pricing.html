Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect immediately and billing is prorated.

What counts as a team?

A team is a group of users sharing the same server registry and configuration. Pro supports up to 3 teams, Enterprise has no limit.

Is there a free trial?

Pro and Enterprise plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

What payment methods do you accept?

PayPal is our primary payment processor. Enterprise customers can also pay via bank transfer upon request.
